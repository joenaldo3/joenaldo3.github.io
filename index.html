More About Joe

Bridging Product and UX

I came into UX through business analysis and product work, where I wrote requirements and documentation. That experience taught me how systems are built and why decisions get made. Over time, I noticed the gap between stakeholder goals and real user behavior. I wanted to shape the experience itself, not just describe it. That’s what led me into UX.
